The by far most populous and the capital city of the [[Rockbit Wastes]], Meadowbite is a hub for all who call the Wastes home. Made of metal scraps and wooden huts, the city is layered, made of 5 individual layers wrapped inside a scrap shell. 

While a large number of inhabitants are travellers making short stays, the footfall is strong enough that the estimated population stays relatively steady and accurate. 

Of those that call Meadowbite a permanent place of residence, the minority are honest, working people getting by. The majority of Meadowbite residence belong to its unforgiving underground criminal world, categorised by rival gangs, large gambling and fighting rings, and constant turf wars that make the lower 4 rings of the city one of the most dangerous parts of the country outside of The Pit. 

The uppermost ring of the city is largely shut off from the public, and not much is known about it publicly.

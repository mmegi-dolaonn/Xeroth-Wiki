The capital city of the [[The Kingdom of Asparath|Kingdom of Asparath]], Solenne is the second largest city in Xeroth, behind the capital city of [[Merovar]], [[Placeholder for Merovar Capital]], but is rapidly growing and building.

Home to a full variety of races, creatures, a rapidly growing local economy, large green spaces, modern shopping markets and an increasingly strong welfare state, the cumulated [[The Lantern-Bearers|Lantern-Bearers]] diaspora came together post-war in an attempt to found a central hub to aid and support the Kingdom in its goals.

## Districts

- City Centre
	The city centre is home to Beating Heart, which is comprised of both the [[The Palace of Solenne|Palace of Solenne]] which is home to the king and his surrounding council, and the [[The Grand Cathedral of Asparath|Grand Cathedral of Asparath]].

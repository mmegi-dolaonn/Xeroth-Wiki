Khardren is the capital and largest city of [[Khaladross]], situated within one of the few major passes through the country's central mountain range. Originally established as a fortified dwarven settlement controlling passage through the mountains, the city expanded considerably as Khaladross developed into Xeroth's leading centre of industry, engineering and military production.

Khardren is built both within and around the mountains themselves. Its oldest districts consist of vast city halls, tunnels and chambers excavated deep into the surrounding rock, while later development has spread outward across the walls of the pass. Bridges connect neighbourhoods built into opposing cliff faces, while lifts, railways and steep roads carry people and materials between the city's different elevations.

One particular lift, [[The Ascent]], has become famous continent-wide due to its incredible engineering - a system of central lifts that the city itself wraps around that facilitates travel between all levels of the mountain.

The lower districts contain much of Khardren's heavy industry. Foundries, workshops and assembly halls operate alongside major freight terminals where ore and goods are transported across Khaladross. Most of the city's military production is also concentrated here, although the most sensitive facilities are maintained deeper within the mountains and are inaccessible to the general public.

Higher within the pass are Khardren's residential and civic districts. Government buildings overlook the industrial valley below, alongside guild halls, military headquarters and some of Khaladross's oldest public institutions. Many of these structures are older than the country's modern industrial expansion and retain the monumental stone architecture traditionally associated with its dwarven population.

Despite its reputation abroad as a city of factories and defenses, Khardren is home to a substantial civilian population. Markets, local businesses, taverns, public halls and neighbourhoods occupy repurposed chambers throughout the older city, while newer districts have developed around the stations and industrial centres constructed during Khaladross's expansion.

### The Unmaking

Khardren was one of Khaladross's most important strategic centres during [[The Unmaking]]. Its location, industrial capacity and extensive underground infrastructure made the city both a major military production centre and one of the most heavily defended settlements on the continent.

Although Khardren itself was never destroyed, the war permanently altered the city. Industrial production expanded rapidly, sections of the mountain were converted into shelters and military facilities, and large numbers of displaced people from elsewhere in Khaladross moved into the capital, contributing to a large amount of overpopulation in lower district residential sections. Many of the military facilities remain in use today.

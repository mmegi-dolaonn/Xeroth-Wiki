Asparath is a Prime Deity who oversees the Peace domain. With the epithet "The Gentle Hand", he is the central idol of worship for members of the [[The Kingdom of Asparath|Kingdom of Asparath]].

## The Unmaking (Misc)

While Asparath's level of direct influence regarding the [[The Lantern-Bearers|Lantern-Bearers]] victory is very publicly debated, it is often agreed that no rebel force would have been able to surmount the previous regime without some level of divine intervention. Most scholars believe that the influence was no more than typically seen through great Paladins and Clerics of years past, and that the victory was largely due to strong tactical manoeuvring and resource allocation, though many inner-city fanatics in [[Solenne]] and beyond would argue otherwise.

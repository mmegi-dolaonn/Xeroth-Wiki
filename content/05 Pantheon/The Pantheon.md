The Pantheon of Gods in use for this campaign follows the Exandrian Pantheon, with a few minor changes. To clarify, this world does not follow Exandrian history or identical cosmology, but does share its pantheon.

The main change is that with the prime deities, the addition of [[Asparath]] has been made.

For details on the Exandrian pantheon for those not familiar, refer to https://criticalrole.miraheze.org/wiki/Deities_of_Exandria, being wary of Critical Role spoilers for anyone interested.

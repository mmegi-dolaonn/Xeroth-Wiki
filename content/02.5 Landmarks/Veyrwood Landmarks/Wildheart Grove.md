A vast and ancient clearing hidden deep within the forests of Veyrwood, the Wildheart Grove is one of the most sacred sites in the nation and a place of pilgrimage for druids from across its forests and wetlands.

At its heart stands an immense iridescent tree, unlike any known species in Xeroth. It shifts subtly between deep greens and gold, while its leaves seem to hold their own inner light. Natural magic pours from the tree almost tangibly, causing the surrounding grove to remain green and in bloom throughout the year. Flowers bloom beyond their seasons, wounded animals have been known to recover much more quickly, and at night the clearing is illuminated by a soft radiance that seems to emanate from the earth itself in warm orange or cold blue tones depending on the season.

The grove serves as the gathering place of the [[The Verdant Creed of Veyrwood|Verdant Creed]], where the leaders of Veyrwood's scattered communities assemble to deliberate on matters concerning the nation as a whole. Meetings beneath the tree carry both political and spiritual significance, with major decisions traditionally made in its presence.

The true nature of the tree remains unknown. Some druids and scholars who have studied the grove widely believe it to be an avenue through which the Wildmother touches the Material Plane and her influence is felt more vividly, whereas others believe that the tree is not just touched by natural magicks, but is itself one of its original sources.

The Maw is the name for the large coastal access point to the south of the continent, comprised mostly of Merovarian coastline, but featuring some of Laerynn. It got its name due to both its appearance, resembling a large open mouth, and also because of a nickname given by locals in [[Saltfront City]] after new taxation laws introduced were "eating away" at local profits.

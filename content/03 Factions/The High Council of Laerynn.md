The High Council of Laerynn is the operating government structure from the eponymous nation. Following old elven tradition while adapting to the new central identity of the continent, they typically favour more conservative attitudes, with the exception of the progression of magic in all of its forms. They recently publicly stated the only reason they agreed to share in their colleges was to "broaden the horizons of magic with new ideas", which caused a small stir among the [[The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity|Coalition]].

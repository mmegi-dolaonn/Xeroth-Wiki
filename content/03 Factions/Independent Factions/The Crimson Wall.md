An individual organisation formed in 848AR following the end of the war, featuring soldiers, mercenaries and guards-for-hire tasked with the sole purpose of policing entry to [[The Pit]], and preventing anything from getting out.

The Crimson Wall was instated almost immediately as [[The Kingdom of Asparath]]'s first policy as a new nation with any influence to [[The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity|The Coalition]], as protecting from the lingering effects of the war remains a priority for the Kingdom.

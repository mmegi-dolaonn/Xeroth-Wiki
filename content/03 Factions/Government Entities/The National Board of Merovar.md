The National Board is the leading council that heads the nation of [[Merovar]]. 

The Board elects a **First Consul** from among its members to serve as the public head of the Merovarian state. Although the Consul possesses considerable authority over diplomacy, military affairs and the administration of government, major decisions concerning taxation, trade and foreign expenditure require the support of the Board. Access to the Board is almost entirely decided on a rolling basis of the richest and most influential figures across each city and district, meaning while a focus on economic prosperity is the central aim of the Board, the farming colonies of the north-west are often under-represented.

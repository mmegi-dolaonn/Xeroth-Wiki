The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity, most commonly referred to as 'The Coalition', is an international organisation of countries founded with the intent to maintain continental peace, protect quality of life, and foster international cooperation.

Current members of the Coalition include:

- [[Merovar]]
- [[Khaladross]]
- [[Laerynn]]

With various individual parties from the [[Rockbit Wastes]] also interacting with the Coalition.

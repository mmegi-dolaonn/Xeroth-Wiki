# Overview
The Lantern-Bearers of Asparath, more commonly known simply by 'The Lantern-Bearers' were the rebel group that later became the [[The Kingdom of Asparath|Kingdom of Asparath]]. 

Named so due to the 'light of hope' they provided to oppressed groups and races during the peak of [[The Unmaking]].

Originally a very small subset of followers of Asparath, they rapidly grew throughout the mid-stages of the war, eventually becoming big enough to rival a nation. After the end of the war, the members from across the nation came together to found the new, centralised kingdom.

title: Ban List
---
A list of non-core/homebrew spells and rules allowed/disallowed.

- With few exceptions (specified later), all official materials from DnD5e source books that appear on dnd5e.wikidot.com are allowed.

- Spells and abilities from the Critical Role source books are also allowed, particularly the Blood Hunter class and relevant subclasses, and Dunamancy (Chronurgy, Graviturgy) spells and subclasses.

- A list of currently banned/omitted are as follows:
	- Silvery Barbs
	- Gunslinger (Fighter)

Homebrew spells/abilities found and requested will be reviewed on an individual basis - as this falls under character-specific requests, a list of already agreed upon homebrew will not feature on the wiki.

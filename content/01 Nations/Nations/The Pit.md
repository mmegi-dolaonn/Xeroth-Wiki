The Pit refers to the neutral area of land that forms the remains of [[The Unmaking]]. The epicentre of the conflict, it is now host to piles of bodies, mutated amalgamations of what was once nature born from twisted battle-magic, and other various mysteries. 

Public access is strictly prohibited, with an individual organisation named [[The Crimson Wall]] formed from soldiers of all members of the continent acting around the border to prevent any access to keep the surrounding areas and people from danger.

The most affluent country in Xeroth, Merovar controls much of the continental trade through its large coastline and access to the Maw, the large coastal line to the south of Xeroth which acts as a major docking hub. Most of the richest and most influential cities in Merovar, such as [[Saltfront City]], lie along the Maw and are home to most of the continent's nobles and country houses for various officials and leaders. 

To the north west of the country further from the Maw are a collection of vineyards and affluent countryside estates, mixed alongside much poorer and destitute farming colonies, with farms making up most of the non-coastal economy and the farming class providing much of what the cities on the coast trade.

## Government 

Merovar is governed as a mercantile republic, with political authority concentrated among the country's noble houses, major trading families, banking institutions and chartered companies. National affairs are overseen by  [[The National Board of Merovar]], whose seats are held by representatives of the country's wealthiest cities and most influential commercial and landed interests.

Merovarian politics is dominated less by ideological divisions than by competing commercial interests, family alliances and disputes between shipping and land. Still, the government places its main emphasis on stability, international cooperation and the continued expansion of trade, choosing to see the success of Merovar as conducive to the success of Xeroth, and vice versa.
## Geography 

Coastal winds and rocky shores to the south, with rolling farmlands and hills to the north west.

## Relations

Maintains a strong trading stance with all nations of Xeroth and worldwide. A major benefactor and core member of [[The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity|The Coalition]], the funds and men provided by Merovar were pivotal to the victory of the Coalition during [[The Unmaking]]. As a result, most countries are in some form of debt to Merovar, with [[The Kingdom of Asparath]] holding the largest share of the debt by a long way. As a result, Merovarian officials hold a large sum of influence over other countries politically, though intentions seem positive and constructive.

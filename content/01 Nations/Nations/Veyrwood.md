Veyrwood occupies the dense forests and wetlands of western Xeroth, stretching across vast ancient woodland, marshes and river valleys that have historically left much of the country difficult to traverse or settle.

Its population is spread between relatively small settlements rather than large cities, with many communities having existed in near isolation for centuries. While Veyrwood is home to people of many ancestries, Firbolgs, Orcs, Elves, Humans, Goblins and other peoples traditionally associated with woodland environments make up much of its population.

Veyrwood culture places significant importance on locality, ancestry and the preservation of the natural environment. Communities commonly view themselves as inhabitants of a particular forest, river or valley before viewing themselves as citizens of a wider nation, and considerable differences in customs exist between different regions.

## Government

Veyrwood is governed as a decentralised confederation of local communities, known collectively as the [[The Verdant Creed of Veyrwood|Verdant Creed]].

Individual settlements, clans and territories govern themselves according to their own traditions. Some are overseen by elected councils, while others recognise hereditary leaders, druids, elders or other local authorities. Provided these communities adhere to a small collection of common laws, the central government rarely interferes with their internal affairs.

Representatives from recognised communities periodically gather at the [[Wildheart Grove]] to make decisions concerning foreign relations and matters affecting Veyrwood as a whole.

The Creed does not appoint one leader from among its representatives to act as Veyrwood's principal diplomat and wartime coordinator, unlike all other nations, as to not diminish any one of the more influential local leaders, and as such maintains its council-like stance in hard times.

This decentralisation is considered by many Veyrwood citizens to be one of the country's defining principles. Consequently, proposals originating from foreign states or the Coalition that would establish continent-wide standards are often met with considerably more scrutiny within Veyrwood than elsewhere.

## Geography

Veyrwood is dominated by enormous forests, wetlands and river systems.

The interior contains some of the oldest woodland on the continent, where enormous canopies allow relatively little sunlight to reach the forest floor. Settlements are commonly constructed around existing natural features rather than clearing them, with roads outside the largest population centres often consisting of dirt paths, raised wooden causeways or navigable rivers.

Towards the south and east, the forests gradually thin into open woodland and grasslands connecting Veyrwood with the centre of Xeroth. The northern regions become colder and rockier as they approach [[Khaladross]], while large areas of the western lowlands consist of bogs, marshland and flooded forest.

The country's difficult terrain has historically made conventional invasion extremely difficult. Local inhabitants are usually considerably more capable of navigating Veyrwood than outsiders, and during periods of conflict its forests have frequently served as natural defensive barriers.

## Relations

During [[The Unmaking]], Veyrwood fought alongside the Greylock Empire. This was primarily the result of longstanding political ties and fears among many Veyrwood communities that the emerging Coalition would eventually interfere with their autonomy. Following the Coalition and the Lantern-Bearers' win in the war, Veyrwood quickly surrendered and withdrew all troops, to harsh penalties and judgements from other countries, despite being allowed to retain its land and stature.

Relations have improved considerably over the following decade.

Veyrwood maintains a largely neutral relationship with [[The Kingdom of Asparath]]. While many within Veyrwood remain incredibly wary of the Kingdom's efforts to build a stronger, centralised Xerothian identity, Asparath's government has generally respected Veyrwood's existing institutions, preventing significant hostility from developing between the two.

Relations with Khaladross, however, remain considerably worse. The two countries fought extensively during the Unmaking, particularly along their northern border, and isolated civilian disputes and occasional unauthorised skirmishes continue despite formal peace between both governments, particularly over Khaladross's machinations destroying a lot of local wildlife, and Veyrwood's natural resources being instrumental to attacks made against Khaladrian civilisation.

Veyrwood trades readily with [[Merovar]], although many communities oppose excessive foreign ownership or exploitation of Veyrwood's natural resources.

Its relationship with [[Laerynn]] has become comparatively positive, particularly through cooperation concerning natural magic, alchemy and the study of magical flora and fauna, though Veyrwood authorities remain protective of sites they consider culturally or ecologically significant.

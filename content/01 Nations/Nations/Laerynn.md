# Nation Title 

Laerynn is the continent's central hub for magical exploration and institution. While they don't hold a monopoly on Wizarding schools or Bard colleges, a vast majority, and certainly the best and most prestigious of those institutions are based in Laerynn. 

Historically an elven nation, it has recently opened up its borders for easier immigration and a higher percentage of non-elven students in line with [[The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity|Coalition]] policies and suggestions made and backed by the [[The Kingdom of Asparath|Kingdom of Asparath]], agreed and voted upon by all members of the Coalition including Laerynn themselves to establish a stronger continental identity. 

## Government 

 Laerynn is governed as a magocratic republic, with a lot of its political authority distributed between its major cities, magical institutions and scholarly bodies. National legislation is debated within [[The High Conclave of Laerynn]], whose members are drawn from representatives of recognised wizarding schools, bardic colleges, arcane guilds, civic governments and other institutions.

The Conclave elects a High Chancellor to act as head of government and chief representative of Laerynn abroad. The Chancellor serves a fixed term and possesses large amounts of  executive authority, though major legislation, military commitments and changes to magical regulation require the approval of the Conclave.

Political divisions within Laerynn most commonly concern the regulation of magical experimentation, the autonomy of academic institutions, access to magical education and the extent to which Laerynn should share its research with the wider continent. 
## Geography 

Laerynn is largely made up of a mix of temperate woodland, open grasslands and gently elevated country. Its largest cities and academic institutions are concentrated around major waterways, lakes, and old centres of elven settlement with smaller towns frequently developing around colleges, archives and magical workshops.

Laerynn’s infrastructure incorporates magic directly into its construction. Permanent teleportation circles, enchanted roads and magically maintained bridges connect many of the country’s major population centres, making travel between its cities considerably easier than the surrounding terrain might otherwise allow.

The more remote parts of Laerynn remain heavily forested, particularly around older elven settlements. These areas contain many abandoned ruins dating from earlier periods of Laerynn’s history, some of which remain under the ownership or supervision of the institutions which originally constructed them.
## Relations

Laerynn is a founding member of [[The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity|The Coalition]] and maintains mostly positive relations with the other members. Its main contribution to the Coalition is academic rather than economic, providing expertise, research and trained spellcasters to Coalition projects.

Relations with [[The Kingdom of Asparath]] have grown considerably since the end of [[The Unmaking]]. Laerynn has supported many of the Kingdom’s attempts to encourage greater continental cooperation, including reforms intended to make travel, immigration and education between Coalition nations easier. The recent expansion of places offered to non-elven students within Laerynn has been one of the most obvious consequences of this.

Laerynn maintains particularly close practical relations with [[Khaladross]], with magical research from Laerynn frequently finding industrial applications there. The relationship is not without disagreement, however, particularly about military research and the extent to which new magical discoveries should be adapted for warfare.

[[Merovar]] remains one of the largest sources of foreign investment into Laerynn’s institutions and one of the primary routes through which Laerynn acquires rare materials and foreign texts. In return, Laerynn’s schools and magical industries provide Merovarian merchants with access to expertise unavailable throughout much of the wider world.

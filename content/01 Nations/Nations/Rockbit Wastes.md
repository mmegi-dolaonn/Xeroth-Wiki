The Rockbit Wastes to the North-east of Xeroth are an independent state comprised of one large sand desert, with a large variety of flora and fauna and limited population. A large majority of the population are located in its one large settlement, [[Meadowbite]]. 
## Government 

The Rockbit Wastes do not operate under a form of government, and are effectively an anarchist state with individual autonomous societies surviving through the desert that collectively refer to and align with the nation and its iconography. Due to this, while individual parties may interact with other states, the country has no central representatives or figureheads, though the capital city [[Meadowbite]] does operate on its own internal hierarchy. 

## Geography 

Hot, rolling desert hills, with ever-changing harsh weather. While mostly sand dunes, the amount of thunderstorms, tornados and other natural phenomena constantly shift local geography and so while larger scale civilisations and cities do exist, most of the country is bare, with smaller towns usually constantly travelling to find resources.

## Relations

As a collection of different factions and groups, it is difficult to say the Wastes themselves hold any defined relations. However, despite not being a member, the Wastes have a decent connection to the [[The Coalition for Xerothian Prosperity|Coalition]]. Many individual parties and travelling groups aided the Coalition in military operations and supplying firepower and resources. 

## Legend

There are rumours of an ancient, vicious sand worm apex predator lurking in the desert sands, but there haven't been any confirmed sightings for centuries. The agreed explanation is that if there ever really was such a creature, it has surely since died.
